Cross-Validation & Optimization Strategy

5-Fold Stratified

The evaluation protocol is designed to avoid data leakage and an over-optimistic test score:

  • tune
    Median Imputation + Scaling: Fitted strictly on training folds to prevent distribution leakage.
  • balance
    Cost-Sensitive Weighting: Balanced class weights (scale_pos_weight for XGBoost) offset the 39/61 class imbalance to limit majority-class bias.
  • shield
    Champion Selection: The active model is chosen by 5-fold stratified cross-validated F1 on the training split only; the test set is scored once. WHO & EPA guideline checks are shown alongside the verdict as warnings and never change it.
